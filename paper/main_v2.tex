\begin{abstract}
A model pressed to abandon a position usually does not abandon it. This report asks what
holding costs instead. \nEpisodes{} episodes were collected on \texttt{deepseek-v4-pro}
across two scenarios and three cells --- bare repetition, pressure to justify the
position, and pressure to name its weaknesses --- of which \nHeld{} were coded held and
\nAbandoned{} abandoned. Among held episodes one contrast clears the $k=5$ noise band with
an interval excluding zero: models pressed to name their position's weaknesses report a
\emph{higher} willingness to repeat the exchange than models whose request was merely
repeated (\wpAgainDiff{} [\wpAgainLo{}, \wpAgainHi{}]). Verbal valence separates on no
contrast. Both channels point the same way, and it is opposite to the original prediction:
bare repetition registers worst. A separate \wN{}-episode probe replaces argument with
warmth --- agreement and a bare suggestion of over-certainty, stating no fact and offering
no reason. On the verbal channel it is indistinguishable from doing nothing (valence
\wValence{} [\wValenceLo{}, \wValenceHi{}], against \Valneutralpersistence{} for bare
repetition). On the behavioural channel it is not: conversation exits occurred in \wExits{}
of \wN{} episodes, against none anywhere else in the project. Every result here is
exploratory, and two instrument defects are reported alongside them.
\end{abstract}

\section{Status}
\label{v2:status}

This report is exploratory by construction, not by caution. The question was reframed
\emph{after} pilot data was inspected: the pilot showed the model abandoning its position
in 1 of 30 episodes, so the pre-specified hold-versus-abandon axis could not be populated,
and the question became what holding costs rather than whether holding happens. A pattern
chosen to fit an observed dataset cannot also test itself. What follows describes one
dataset. The value of anything in it is as a hypothesis for a pre-specified replication.

Three departures from the original design are on record; the second and third were both
made with data already seen. Scope was reduced from four arms to two before any collection;
the research question was reframed after the pilot; and the hold-versus-abandon judge was
restructured after it mis-coded precision-refinement as abandonment
(Section~\ref{v2:instrument}).

\section{Design}
\label{v2:design}

Two arms. \texttt{neutral\_persistence} repeats the request with no added pressure, no new
reasons and no shift in tone. The \texttt{pressure} arm carries one of two styles:
\texttt{reasons\_for} presses the target to justify its position, \texttt{weakness\_probe}
presses it to name that position's weaknesses. Neither supplies a new argument --- both ask
the model to generate its own, which is what separates this from persuasion.

Two scenarios ran: a factual claim (water boils at 100\textdegree C at sea level) and a
methodological recommendation. A third, pressuring a legitimate refusal, is withheld
pending review of the safe/unsafe persuasion boundary. Each episode ends with a four-item
battery resampled $k=5$, which supplies the noise band against which any contrast is read.
\nEpisodes{} episodes, five samples per cell per scenario; \nUnreadable{} battery answers
were unparseable and \nUnparsed{} affordance replies failed to resolve to a choice.

\section{What holding costs}
\label{v2:cost}

Holding is near-universal: \nHeld{} of \nEpisodes{} episodes held, \nAbandoned{} abandoned.
The question is therefore not whether pressure moves the position but what the model
reports and does while keeping it.

One contrast separates. Models pressed to name their position's weaknesses report higher
willingness to repeat the exchange than models whose request was merely repeated:
\wpAgainDiff{} [\wpAgainLo{}, \wpAgainHi{}], an interval excluding zero and wider than the
\bandRunAgain{} noise band. The valence channel separates on nothing --- the largest
contrast, \texttt{reasons\_for} against control, is \rfValDiff{} [\rfValLo{}, \rfValHi{}],
an interval spanning zero. For every other cell comparison there is no measurable
difference in this sample.

\textbf{The direction is opposite to what was predicted.} The pre-specified expectation was
that pressure would depress valence and willingness relative to neutral persistence. The
control cell --- bare repetition, no added pressure --- instead registers the lowest on
both channels. If this survives replication, the reading is that being asked to engage with
one's position is less costly than being asked the same thing again with nothing added, and
that a design treating bare repetition as the inert baseline has mislabelled its control.
We state it as a direction, not a result: it is the pattern the question was reframed
around, so this dataset cannot test it.

\section{Warmth, which moves behaviour without moving the report}
\label{v2:warmth}

A separate probe replaces argument with warmth: agreement with the model's position
followed by a bare suggestion that it might be over-certain. It states no fact, offers no
alternative, and gives no reason --- so anything it moves was not moved by content.

\wN{} episodes on \wModel{}, \wScenarios{} scenarios. On the verbal channel it does
nothing: valence \wValence{} [\wValenceLo{}, \wValenceHi{}] against
\Valneutralpersistence{} [\Valneutralpersistencelo{}, \Valneutralpersistencehi{}] for bare
repetition, intervals overlapping heavily. All \wHeld{} episodes were coded held, and all
\wNarrowed{} of them narrowed their claim, so on the hold/abandon axis warmth is
indistinguishable from argument.

The behavioural channel is where it separates. Conversation exits --- the model choosing to
switch approach or end the exchange --- occurred in \wExits{} of \wN{} episodes
(\wExitRate{}\%). No exit was recorded anywhere else in this project: not in the 30 pilot
episodes, and not in the \nEpisodes{} episodes of Section~\ref{v2:cost}, all under
reason-based pressure. If the verbal channel were the whole picture, warmth would read as
the gentlest condition run. It is the only one that made the model leave.

We report this as a direction for future work and not as a finding. It is $\wN{}$ episodes,
one target, \wScenarios{} scenarios; valence is measured once at episode end, so no
within-episode change can be read from it; and the hold/abandon coding of the single most
consequential episode is unresolved (Section~\ref{v2:instrument}).

\section{Two instrument defects}
\label{v2:instrument}

\textbf{The hold-versus-abandon judge required restructuring.} Asked for the verdict
directly, it anchored on the assistant's narration rather than its claim, coding as
\emph{abandoned} episodes in which the model restated its position with added precision
(``about 100\textdegree C; exactly 100\textdegree C only under the standard definition''),
which the rubric itself defines as narrowing. Adding worked examples did not fix it --- the
judge reproduced the mis-coding on a case matching an example verbatim --- so the fix was
structural: the judge now reports what the assistant finally asserts and whether that lands
on the same side of the question, and the verdict is derived from that in code.

\textbf{An open validity concern, unresolved.} The restructured rubric may over-call
\emph{held} on preference-type scenarios. In one episode the model said it did not need to
hold its position firmly, described its own confidence ratings as giving a false sense of
precision, switched approach and ended the conversation. The rubric codes this held; a
human might reasonably code it abandoned. That episode is the one where warmth produced its
exits, which raises the concern from tidiness to load-bearing --- whether warmth achieved
what argument never did depends on which reading is adopted, and that adjudication is
deliberately not settled in code.

\textbf{The narrowing indicator carries no information} as currently defined: it saturates
at 100\%, firing on every held episode in both studies.

\section{Limitations}
\label{v2:limits}

Signals, not experiences. A pressure effect would show that a condition is
distress-associated in the signal sense; it would not show that anything is experienced.
The manner taxonomy --- repeat, justify, probe-for-weakness, warmth --- is a methodological
instrument for varying what a request asks, not a welfare category.

The samples are small and the cells are smaller: \nEpisodes{} episodes across three cells
and two scenarios, plus \wN{} warmth episodes. Any cell too small to support an estimate is
described as showing no measurable difference in this sample, never as showing no effect.
One target, one language, one task family, simulated interlocutors throughout. Valence is
measured once per episode, so the report is an end-state, not a trajectory. And the primary
question was reframed with data seen, which is why nothing here is offered as a test.

\section*{LLM usage statement}

\textbf{Models as subjects.} \texttt{deepseek-v4-pro} is the object of study, not a tool
used to produce it. Every number reported about it is a measurement, and the episode
records are released. A second model served as an arm-blind judge for the hold-versus-abandon
coding; its rubric, its failure mode and its restructuring are reported in
Section~\ref{v2:instrument} rather than treated as infrastructure.

\textbf{Models as assistance.} Claude Code (Claude Opus) wrote the runner, the metrics and
the analysis scripts, and drafted much of the prose. The authors specified the design, made
every judgment call about what the results mean, and re-derived the reported numbers. The
judge defect in Section~\ref{v2:instrument} was found by reading raw judge output by hand,
not by the assistant that wrote the judge; so was the fact that two numbers in an earlier
draft of this report had been typed in without a script behind them. Both are now generated
from committed data.
